# Report output patterns

Use the school's official report form when provided. The sections below are FALLBACK structures, not mandatory replacements for approved templates. Render in polished Vietnamese with the official organization name, the verified period and clear source references. Do not fill a section with manufactured achievements simply to complete it.

## Monthly Center report

1. Document heading: unit, reporting month, inclusive dates, cutoff, DRAFT status, source list.
2. Executive overview: 3-5 outcomes that have clear evidence; avoid general praise.
3. Implementation results by workstream: MANAGEMENT, IT_DX, COMMUNICATIONS, STATISTICS, EDU_SUPPORT. Each: notable activities, tangible products, verified quantities with units, plan task ID and evidence source.
4. Plan-vs-actual table: item, owner, expected, actual, evidence, progress/variance, next action.
5. Extra-plan activities, explicitly separated from plan compliance.
6. Open/blocked items and coordination requests, with responsible party and decision needed where known.
7. Next period priorities: grounded in uncompleted plans or approved next-month assignments.
8. Data quality / sources / approval and changes register.

## Quarterly report

State the official start and end dates, individual subperiods and whether they overlap. Summarize key outputs once, show period-over-period comparisons only for comparable periods, list workstream achievements and lingering risks, and propose specific management decisions. Never infer a calendar quarter from a school operating month.

## School-year / annual Center report

Organize around approved Center functions, full-year result metrics and evidence, beyond-assignment contributions, governance and system improvements, resource limitations with realistic corrective measures, then next-year priorities. Use same-year evidence only. Distinguish calendar-year staff assessment from school-year collective reporting when instructions differ.

## Personnel and collective evaluation

For an individual: tasks assigned, verified outcomes, impact when documented, limits and corrective measures. For the Center: aggregate the outcomes once, explain collaboration, do not claim individual achievements twice. If forms PL I / PL II / PL III are supplied, map content to those exact forms; don't assert a rating or invent evidence. Only a competent school authority finalizes classification.

## Leadership oral brief (1-2 minutes)

- Most important verified result and its meaning.
- One risk or structural bottleneck and why it matters.
- One decision/coordination request, specifying who is asked and desired outcome.

No long generic introduction. If no decision is needed, say this is informational only.

## Audit of an existing report

Return (a) errors and contradictions ranked by severity; (b) exact source/value disagreements; (c) proposed replacement wording, preserving source uncertainty; (d) missing workstreams and evidence; (e) status and approval requirements. Never silently rewrite the user's file.
